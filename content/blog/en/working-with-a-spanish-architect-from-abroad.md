If you live in the United Kingdom, Scandinavia, the Netherlands, Germany or elsewhere and plan a villa on the Costa del Sol, some things will work as you expect and others will not. Knowing how a project is organised in Spain helps you take part with confidence.

## The architect's role in Spain

In Spain, the project for a home must be drawn up by an architect, and the same professional usually stays involved during the works. Law 38/1999 on Building distinguishes the **site director**, who directs the works in their technical, aesthetic and planning aspects, from the **director of the execution of the works**, usually a building engineer (arquitecto técnico), who directs the material execution.

## Who builds

The building contractor is a separate company, contracted by the client. The architect can help prepare the documentation to compare offers and coordinate the companies involved, according to the scope agreed. It is worth being clear from the start about who does what.

## Permissions

Planning permission is granted by the town council under the regional and municipal rules. In Marbella and the rest of Andalusia, the framework is Law 7/2021 (LISTA). Our guide on the [building licence in Marbella](/journal/building-licence-in-marbella) explains the steps.

## Language and documents

Ask in which language meetings, drawings and reports will be. Official documents are submitted in Spanish, but the conversation and the explanations can be in English. Our studio works in English and Spanish.

## Decisions at a distance

Decide at the start how decisions will be shared: video calls, drawings and visualisations sent in advance, and a clear record of what has been agreed. Plan your visits for the moments when being there adds most, such as key design meetings and important stages of the works.

## Time and patience

Projects in Spain go through design, permissions and works, and each stage takes its time. Ask for a realistic plan for your project rather than general figures.

## Questions for a first conversation

1. Who will be my point of contact, and in which language?
2. What does the commission include, and what will I contract separately?
3. How will I follow the works from abroad?
4. When is it worth being in Spain?
5. How are changes during the works agreed?
