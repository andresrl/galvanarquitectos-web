Building a villa or carrying out a major renovation in Marbella requires the municipal authorisation that corresponds to the works. This guide explains the general framework and the steps that are usually involved. It is not legal advice: each project is checked against the regulations in force when it is prepared.

## The framework in Andalusia

Since December 2021, urban planning in Andalusia is governed by Law 7/2021 on the sustainability of the territory (LISTA), which replaced the previous law (LOUA). Its general regulation was approved by Decree 550/2022.

LISTA combines three ways of authorising works: the **urban planning licence**, the **responsible declaration** (declaración responsable) and the **prior communication** (comunicación previa). Which one applies depends on the type of work. A new villa, or works affecting the structure or the volume of a house, generally require a project and a licence; smaller works may follow simpler procedures. The architect determines which applies in each case.

## The municipal rules in Marbella

Each municipality applies its own planning rules. In Marbella, the Town Council and the Málaga Association of Architects have proposed that new-build licence applications include a document justifying compliance with the planning regulations, to make the municipal review faster. Among other information, it asks for topographic plans that identify the existing trees and which of them are kept, cut down or transplanted.

## Who drafts the project

Under Law 38/1999 on Building (LOE), the project for a residential building must be drawn up by an architect. The same law distinguishes the **site director** (director de obra), who directs the works in their technical, aesthetic, planning and environmental aspects, from the **director of the execution of the works**, who directs their material execution.

## The usual steps

1. **Preliminary study**: what the plot or the house allows under the applicable rules.
2. **Basic project**: the design and the documentation needed to apply for the licence.
3. **Application and municipal review**: the technical services check the project; they may ask for clarifications.
4. **Execution project**: the detailed documentation for building.
5. **Works**: directed and supervised by the site management until completion.
