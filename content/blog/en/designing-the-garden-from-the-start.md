On the Costa del Sol, much of life in a villa happens outdoors: on the terrace, by the pool, in the shade of a pergola. Yet the garden is often considered last, when the house has already been decided and fewer options remain.

Designing the exterior from the start does not mean choosing every plant on day one. It means the house and the garden are conceived as a single composition.

## What is lost by leaving it until last

When the garden comes late, it adapts to whatever is left instead of being part of the project. Some decisions that affect the exterior are made very early:

- **Where the house opens to the outdoors**, and from which rooms.
- **At what level the terraces sit** in relation to the ground.
- **Where the pool goes** and how you reach it.
- **Where services run**, which will later affect planting or irrigation.

If these decisions are made without thinking about the garden, they are harder to correct afterwards.

## Outdoor spaces are rooms too

A good exterior is organised like the interior: with places to sit, eat, rest or gather, and clear paths between them. Ask yourself how you will use the garden through the day and through the year:

- Where would you like to have breakfast or lunch outside?
- Which areas need shade in summer?
- Which part of the garden do you want to be more private?
- How do you move from the house to the pool?

## Planting, shade and water

Planting, shade and water needs depend on the place: orientation, exposure to wind, the ground and how you want to use the garden. There is no list of species that suits every plot.

That is why they are worth studying for each project rather than applying standard solutions. If maintenance or water use concerns you, say so from the start so it can be considered in the design.

## If the garden already exists

In a renovation, the existing garden can be an excellent starting point. Mature trees, walls or areas that work well deserve to be assessed before everything is redone. Keeping what makes sense and rethinking the rest applies to the outdoors too.

## A service on its own or alongside the architecture

Landscape design can be commissioned independently or developed together with the architecture and interiors. What matters is that the design of the exterior is in dialogue with the house.
